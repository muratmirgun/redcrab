Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

redcrab is a Redis and Valkey client made by Murat Mirgün Ercan. It collects no data about you.

The app

  • No accounts, analytics, tracking or ads. redcrab sends nothing about you or your use of it to its author or to anyone else.
  • Your connections stay on your Mac. Connection settings are saved in redcrab's own folder; passwords and passphrases in the macOS Keychain. Files you pick, such as an SSH key or a TLS certificate, are copied into that folder so redcrab can read them again. None of it leaves your Mac.
  • It connects only where you tell it. redcrab talks to the Redis, Valkey and SSH servers you set up. The copy from the Mac App Store contacts nothing else; the App Store updates it. Copies downloaded from GitHub also ask GitHub's API once a day whether there is a newer release, sending nothing about you.

Removing redcrab and its folder removes everything it saved; its Keychain entries are listed under "redcrab" in Keychain Access.

This website

This site has no cookies, analytics or trackers. It is a set of static pages; the company hosting it may keep the usual short-lived server logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect its service.

Questions

Open an issue at github.com/muratmirgun/redcrab/issues. If this policy changes, the new one is posted here with a new date.
