# redcrab privacy policy

*Last updated: 11 November 2026*

redcrab is a Redis and Valkey client made by Murat Mirgün Ercan. It collects no data
about you.

- **No accounts, analytics, tracking or ads.** redcrab sends nothing about you or your
  use of it to its author or to anyone else.
- **Your connections stay on your Mac.** Connection settings are saved in redcrab's
  own folder; passwords and passphrases in the macOS Keychain. Files you pick, such
  as an SSH key or a TLS certificate, are copied into that folder so redcrab can read
  them again. None of it leaves your Mac.
- **It connects only where you tell it.** redcrab talks to the Redis, Valkey and SSH
  servers you set up. The copy from the Mac App Store contacts nothing else; the App
  Store updates it. Copies downloaded from GitHub also ask GitHub's API once a day
  whether there is a newer release, sending nothing about you.

Removing redcrab and its folder removes everything it saved; its Keychain entries are
listed under "redcrab" in Keychain Access.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/muratmirgun/redcrab/issues.
